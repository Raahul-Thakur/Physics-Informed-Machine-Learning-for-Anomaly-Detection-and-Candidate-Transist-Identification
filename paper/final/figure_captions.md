# Figure captions and table guide

All figures are supplied as PNG and PDF. Diagnostic figures use retained photometry, not unfiltered raw cadences. Catalogue labels are reference annotations, not conclusions from the images.

1. **Sample selection (`sample_selection`).** Requested, selected and analysed targets in development and independent evaluation. False-positive catalogue availability limits selected counts; selected and analysed counts coincide after successful retries. See `sample_selection_attrition.csv` for exact counts.
2. **Independent recovery (`independent_recovery`).** Catalogue-host recall versus inspection budget for BLS, compact ensemble and compact hybrid. All-sector and coverage-selected single-sector analyses are separate. Lines connect evaluated budgets and do not imply measurements at intervening budgets. Denominators include all 300 catalogue-positive hosts.
3. **Feature-family comparison (`hybrid_feature_comparisons`).** Hybrid top-100 host recovery for the six frozen representations. The left panel is development calibration-target evidence (200 targets); the right is independent evaluation (924 targets). Dashed lines indicate each population's BLS result. Absolute bars across panels are not estimates on the same population.
4. **Paired primary uncertainty (`paired_primary_uncertainty`).** Hybrid-minus-BLS recovered-host differences and percentile 95% intervals from 5,000 paired target bootstrap replicates. Both methods are reranked in each replicate. Training and calibration are fixed, so this is not total model-development uncertainty. The intervals include zero and do not establish an improvement.
5. **Injection pilot (`injection_comparison`).** Direct-period retrieval fraction and median paired depth bias by injected depth, pooled across the fixed period/duration grid and six real source curves. Each detrending method processes the same 108 signals. The small pilot is not a survey-completeness or false-alarm-calibration experiment.
6. **Scores and coverage (`independent_scores_and_coverage`).** Method-specific maximum target scores and usable sector counts by reference population. BLS and hybrid maxima need not come from the same sector. Unlabelled backgrounds are not true negatives; population overlap is not evidence of astrophysical identity.
7. **Search eligibility (`search_eligibility`).** Representative catalogue periods for 300 hosts: 192 within the effective search interval, 94 known but outside it, and 14 unavailable. This is grid eligibility, not visibility of predicted transits through gaps or completeness over all planets in a system.
8. **Complementarity (`complementarity`).** At budget 100, compare BLS with 80 BLS selections plus 20 additional compact-ensemble selections. The lists contain unique targets. The complementary list recovers fewer hosts in both exposure analyses; gains and losses are in `independent_complementarity.csv`.
9. **Diagnostic supplement (`diagnostics/`).** Ten examples selected by the documented rank/TIC rule. Each panel contains retained photometry, detrended flux, the BLS periodogram, full phase fold, transit zoom and measured metadata. Full-phase bins can dilute narrow dips; time-domain coverage and zoomed plots must also be inspected. Recorded observations and limitations are in `diagnostic_review.md`.

Primary numerical tables are `independent_metrics.csv`, `paired_primary_uncertainty.csv` and `independent_complementarity.csv`. Development tables are explicitly prefixed `development_` or `original_development_`. Feature definitions, selection decisions, preprocessing/search settings, split IDs, eligibility and sampled sectors are exported separately. Bootstrap draws and individual injection trials retain the underlying descriptive measurements.
