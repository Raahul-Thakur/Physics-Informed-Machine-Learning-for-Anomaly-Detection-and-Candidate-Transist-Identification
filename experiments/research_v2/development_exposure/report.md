# Development calibration targets, all sectors — not independent

Scored 200 targets / 269 curves.

Prespecified primary family: compact. Other families are comparisons; do not select a new winner using independent results.

Training and calibration exclude evaluation targets in independent mode. Labels are used only in metrics. The choice of the compact experiment was informed by earlier labelled development results; model fitting and redundancy selection remain unsupervised.

One-sector selection uses most valid cadences, then longest baseline, then smallest sector number, without labels or scores. Models are unchanged between exposure comparisons.

Eligible-positive recall retains the entire inspection pool and changes only the positive denominator. It uses each host's representative period and the effective per-sector grid cap, not epoch visibility or completeness across all planets. Out-of-grid and missing-period hosts remain in full metrics.

Complementarity uses the frozen BLS allocation plus additional anomaly-ranked unique targets. Bootstrap intervals are conditional on those fixed lists, not model-refitting uncertainty. Catalogue recovery is not validated planet purity or measured human inspection time.

Development calibration results are not independent evidence. Unlabelled high-ranked objects remain inspection candidates. Feature proxies are neither causal attributions nor calibrated significance.
